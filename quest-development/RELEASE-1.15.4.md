# PokeTech Arcana Quest Book 1.15.4

Reworks descriptions and rewards across the complete quest book.

- All 3751 quests now explain how to obtain or craft their targets and where those targets are useful.
- Rewards use chapter-specific pools and provide modest resources that help with the current stage.
- Animal farm quests provide two matching spawn eggs, while early island quests provide expansion materials.
- Finished machines, artifacts, boss trophies, legendary items, and other high-value shortcuts are never granted.
- Task items are never repeated as their own rewards.
- All quest, task, and reward IDs remain stable, preserving player progress.
