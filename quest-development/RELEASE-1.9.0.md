# PokeTech Arcana quests 1.9.0

Adds three approved progression chapters to the server quest book:

- **Apotheosis: Кузница героя** — 76 quests covering affixes, reforging, gems, bosses, and advanced equipment progression.
- **Cataclysm: Цитадели боссов** — 70 quests arranged as distinct expedition routes through the mod's structures and bosses.
- **Draconic Evolution: Сердце хаоса** — 88 quests covering power tiers, fusion crafting, energy storage, reactors, and chaos progression.

The chapters use different map shapes and their own high contrast book backgrounds while keeping the established PokeTech Arcana visual language.

Package contents are generated by `build_book_quests.py` and include all 19 quest chapters, client resources, localization, and the server installation layout.
