# PokeTech Arcana quests 1.11.0

Server quest-book update with three new beginner-friendly chapters:

- **Artifacts: карта потерянных сокровищ** — 72 quests across exploration, mimic dungeons, movement, survival, combat, utility and collection routes.
- **Relics: музей живых реликвий** — 80 quests arranged as a central workshop with independent museum halls and a final curator collection.
- **Epic Fight: школы боевого мастерства** — 90 quests covering combat basics, four weapon schools, Fight or Flight, a mastery exam and tournament progression.

The generated FTB Quests book now contains 25 chapters and 1,841 quests. Item objectives for the three new chapters were checked against the exact mod JAR versions shipped in the pack.

Server package: `PokeTechArcana-quests-book-1.11.0.zip`.
